Start here

A quick guide for the Small Landlord Command Center. The "Make a copy" link and the binder PDF are in your Gumroad purchase. This page explains how to use them.

1. Copy the Sheet (1 minute)

  1. Open the "Make a copy" link from your Gumroad purchase. A computer is easiest for this.
  2. Sign in to your Google account if asked, then click Make a copy.
  3. The copy is saved in your own Google Drive. Only you can see it unless you share it.

2. Clear the example rows

Every tab starts with fictional example rows for "12 Maple St (example), Unit A". They're labeled EXAMPLE DATA - fictional - delete before use.

  1. Look at the Summary tab once, so you can see how the example rows feed the totals.
  2. On the Rent, Expenses and Repairs tabs, select the example rows (row 2 down), right-click and choose Delete rows. Keep row 1, the headers.
  3. Don't delete or rename the tabs. The Summary formulas use the names Rent, Expenses, Repairs and Summary.

3. Everyday use

  • Rent: add one row per unit per month. Mark Late as Y or N.
  • Expenses: pick a category from the dropdown. If a category isn't in the list, use Other. The Summary's "uncategorized check" line should stay at 0.
  • Repairs: log repairs here only, not in Expenses. The Summary adds repairs separately, so logging them in both places would count them twice.
  • More units or properties: just add rows. The formulas read whole columns. v1 totals all properties together.

4. Year-end summary

  1. Open the Summary tab.
  2. Type the year you want in cell B1, for example 2026.
  3. Rent collected, expenses by category, repair costs, total costs, net and the late-payment count all update for that year.

The categories are only for organizing. Ask a tax professional how to report anything.

5. Printable binder

The binder PDF (US Letter, 7 pages) has a cover, property sheet, tenant contact sheet, move-in/move-out inspection checklist (2 pages), maintenance request log and rent receipt log. Print the pages you need. The inspection checklist is meant to be printed once per move-in and once per move-out.

Something not working?

If a Summary cell shows an error, the most common cause is a renamed tab or a misspelled category. Check that the tab names are exactly Rent, Expenses, Repairs and Summary, and that categories come from the dropdown. For anything else, reply to your Gumroad receipt email.
