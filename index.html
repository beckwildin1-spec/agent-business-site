Google Sheets trackers for small businesses

Simple spreadsheets that live in your own Google account. Two trackers are in progress. Both are coming soon on Etsy.

Private Practice Billing & Income Tracker

A Google Sheets tracker for solo therapists and counselors in private practice: sessions, payments, insurance claim status and expenses, with a yearly summary worked out by formula.

Built for client codes, not names. Every tab says: "Do not enter client names or health information; use codes only." The Client Code column accepts only codes like C-001.
Coming soon on Etsy

Not on sale yet. This button goes live when the Etsy listing is published.

What's included

  • Sessions: date, client code, service, in person or telehealth, fee, payer type, attendance (attended, late cancel, no-show). Paid to Date and Balance Due fill in on their own.
  • Payments: client and insurance payments, each linked to a session.
  • Insurance Claims Status: date submitted, status (Submitted, Pending, Paid, Denied, Resubmitted…), days open, follow-up date.
  • Expenses: category dropdown, vendor, amount, receipt link.
  • Yearly Summary: sessions, fees charged, payments received, open balances, claim counts, expenses by category, net and a month-by-month table. Change one cell to switch the year.
  • An Instructions tab and labeled example data. You get a "Make a copy" link, so the Sheet lives in your own Google account.

What it is not

  • Not HIPAA-compliant software, an EHR or a billing service. We make no compliance claim.
  • No fields for names, dates of birth, member IDs, diagnoses or session notes.
  • No tax, legal or billing advice. Expense categories are only for organizing.

Small Landlord Command Center

A Google Sheets tracker for rent, expenses and repairs, with a yearly summary and a printable binder. Made for DIY landlords with 1–4 units.

Coming soon on Etsy

Not on sale yet. This button goes live when the Etsy listing is published.

What's included

  • Rent tab: one row per month per unit: rent due, amount paid, date paid, late Y/N.
  • Expenses tab: date, property, category (dropdown), amount, vendor, receipt link.
  • Repairs tab: date reported, unit, issue, vendor, cost, date fixed, status.
  • Yearly Summary tab: rent collected, expenses by category, repair costs, total costs and net for the year, all calculated by formula.
  • Printable binder (PDF, US Letter, 7 pages): property sheet, tenant contact sheet, move-in/move-out inspection checklist, maintenance request log and rent receipt log.
