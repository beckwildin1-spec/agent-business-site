Private Practice Billing & Income Tracker

A Google Sheets template for solo therapists and counselors. Log sessions, payments, insurance claim status and expenses in one file. Your yearly totals are worked out by formula.

Built for client codes, not names. Every tab says "use codes only." The Client Code column accepts only codes like C-001, so a typed name is rejected. Client codes reduce what's stored but don't make records de-identified.

What's in the file

  • Sessions: date, client code, service, in person or telehealth, minutes, fee, payer type, attendance. Paid to Date and Balance Due fill in on their own.
  • Payments: client and insurance payments, each linked to a session.
  • Insurance Claims Status: date submitted, amount billed, status (Not submitted, Submitted, Pending, Paid, Partially paid, Denied, Resubmitted, Written off), insurer paid, days open, follow-up date.
  • Expenses: 10 categories (rent/office, software & EHR, liability insurance, licensing & CE and more), vendor, amount, receipt link.
  • Yearly Summary: sessions, fees, adjustments, balance due, payments by source, claim counts, expenses by category, net, and a month-by-month table. Change the year in one cell.
  • Instructions tab and labeled, fictional example rows that you delete before you start.

How you get it

  1. Buy on Etsy and download the PDF. It's a digital download, so nothing ships.
  2. Open the link in the PDF on a computer and click Make a copy.
  3. The tracker saves to your own Google Drive. You need a free Google account. We never see what you enter.

What it is not

  • Not HIPAA-compliant software, an EHR or a billing service. We make no compliance claim of any kind.
  • No fields for names, dates of birth, member IDs, diagnoses or session notes.
  • It doesn't submit claims, take payments or connect to your bank or EHR.
  • No tax, legal, billing or coding advice. Expense categories are only for organizing.
  • Built for Google Sheets. Not tested in Excel or Numbers.
