What's included

  • Rent tab: one row per month per unit: rent due, amount paid, date paid, late Y/N.
  • Expenses tab: date, property, category (dropdown), amount, vendor, receipt link.
  • Repairs tab: date reported, unit, issue, vendor, cost, date fixed, status.
  • Yearly Summary tab: rent collected, expenses by category, repair costs, total costs and net for the year, all calculated by formula. Type a different year in one cell to see that year.
  • Printable binder (PDF, US Letter, 7 pages): property sheet, tenant contact sheet, move-in/move-out inspection checklist, maintenance request log and rent receipt log.
  • You get a "Make a copy" link, so the Sheet lives in your own Google account.

What it is not

  • No leases, eviction notices or state-specific legal forms.
  • No tax advice. Expense categories are only for keeping things organized.
  • It's not property-management software. It's a spreadsheet you keep and edit yourself.
