What's in the file

  • Dashboard: total planned, booked, paid so far, still owed, under or over budget (red when over), a wedding-date countdown, your next unpaid payment, RSVP counts and checklist progress. The only thing you type is your wedding date.
  • Budget: starter categories (Venue, Catering, Photography, Attire, Flowers & decor, Music, Other) with a planned amount each. You can rename them or add your own.
  • Vendors & Payments Due: each payment (a deposit or final balance, for example) gets its own row with amount, due date and a Paid checkbox.
  • Guest List: party name, household, side, people in party, RSVP, meal choice, table number and a thank-you-sent checkbox.
  • Checklist and a short Instructions tab. Example rows are fictional and marked EXAMPLE.

How you get it

  1. Buy on Etsy and download the PDF. It's a digital download, so nothing ships.
  2. Open the link in the PDF on a computer and click Make a copy.
  3. The sheet saves to your own Google Drive, and you can share it with your partner. You need a free Google account. We never see what you enter.

What it is not

  • No seating chart, timeline or day-of schedule. Guests don't RSVP through it, because it's for your own tracking.
  • Built for Google Sheets in a web browser. There's no Excel or Numbers version, and it hasn't been tested on phones.
  • A planning tool only, not financial advice. Totals are only as accurate as the amounts you enter.
